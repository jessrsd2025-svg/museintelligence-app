## Purpose

The MuseIntelligence ICD203 app is a reading-room and governance app for the LIBERTAS // VERITAS // AEQUITAS system. It ingests documents and shared AI conversations, renders them as a browsable markdown wiki/index, and governs every claim with provenance, epistemic tags, and the 18-gate acceptance checklist.

*Sources: `SRC-GEMINI-ICD203-2026-11-23` — epistemic tag: SOURCE_REPORTED.*

## Architecture

Observe → Orient → Decide → Act (OODA), with the Capture → Analyze → Strategize → Execute pipeline. The OBSERVE panel supplies the observation tools; the 18-gate checklist is the decision gate for everything the app ships.

*Sources: `SRC-GEMINI-ICD203-2026-11-23` — epistemic tag: SOURCE_REPORTED.*

## Runtime model — open-source backend

The app's engine is Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (Apache 2.0, Hugging Face), loaded only at a commit SHA pinned at build time. Google AI Studio is the build environment only.
